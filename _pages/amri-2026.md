<h2>AMRI 2026</h2>

<div style="text-align: justify;" markdown="1">

**When:** Monday 23rd November 2026.  
**Where:** Vivi Täckholm, Stockholm University.

AMRI will hold a one-day meeting at Stockholm University, ahead of the [Nordic Marine Science Meeting](https://shf.se/en/havsforskningsdagarna/nordisk-havsforskarmote-2026/).

<h3>Registration and abstracts</h3>

Registration is now open, and we are inviting abstracts for talks at AMRI 2026. We particularly encourage PhD students and postdoctoral researchers to submit an abstract and share their work with the AMRI community.

The programme and schedule will be announced once abstracts have been reviewed.

Questions? Get in touch with this year’s [organising committee](/events/amri-2026/#meet-the-amri-2026-organising-committee).

**Registration and abstract submission are open until 23 October 2026.**
